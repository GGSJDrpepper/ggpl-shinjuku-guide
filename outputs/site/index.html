GoodGame Poker Live Shinjuku

Welcome to GoodGame Poker Live Shinjuku. We are part of one of Japan's largest poker groups, and visitors from around the world can enjoy ring games and tournaments in the heart of Tokyo.

Real-money poker gambling is illegal in Japan; amusement poker is played without cash betting or cash-out.

Web Coin guide

Top-finishing players will be offered a player contract with Global Point LLC as a prize. This is a service outsourcing agreement. If you would like to apply Web Coin toward an entry fee, you can use it directly at the time of entry.

Player contract

Players who wish to receive Web Coin or support based on their results must complete a player contract procedure with Global Point LLC.

Eligible support

Web Coin can be used as support for domestic and international travel expenses, tournament fees, and accommodation expenses.
